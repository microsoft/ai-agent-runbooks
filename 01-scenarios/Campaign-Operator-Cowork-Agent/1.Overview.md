# Campaign Operator Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Campaign execution and content governance  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, configured skills and optional plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Excel Online  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; source qualification, governance and pilot acceptance required  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archive available; not tenant-validated

Audit content drift, coordinate campaigns and asset production, prepare event execution views and synthesise customer signals into marketing actions.

**Primary persona:** Campaign, marketing operations, brand/content or field/event marketing manager.

Review-ready marketing execution, not autonomous publishing, source-asset editing or permission to expose customer transcripts.

## Skill and Output Scope

| Skill | Responsibility and expected output |
|---|---|
| `campaign-operator-concierge` | Front door and router: maps a broad campaign goal to exactly one Campaign Operator skill and its trigger phrase; performs no work itself. Required output: One matched skill, why it fits, and the trigger phrase |
| `content-drift-audit` | Point-in-time compliance check of a governed asset package against approved messaging, the brand guide, and manager brand guidance in Teams. Required output: Sortable Excel remediation report + per-owner Teams drafts |
| `campaign-command-center` | Anchor execution view: kickoff, weekly status, blockers, approvals, and optional performance loop. Required output: Kickoff deck + Excel tracker + weekly RAG status + nudges + optimization actions |
| `asset-production` | Brief-to-final asset workflow with review routing and approval tracking. Required output: Asset tracker + versioned drafts + publishing checklist + draft review requests |
| `event-command-center` | Live execution view for speakers, customer meetings, content, social, and follow-ups. Required output: Readiness tracker + meeting briefs + social plan + daily digest + follow-up drafts |
| `customer-signal-synthesis` | Turns surveys, social listening, website feedback, emails, transcripts, and campaign performance into a PowerPoint activation brief with 3-5 prioritized actions and a draft Teams update. Required output: PowerPoint activation brief (3-5 actions) + draft Teams update |

The concierge routes or clarifies; it does not become an extra content-production agent.
The [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) records the actual archive inventory and version
differences. Skill descriptions are delivery contracts, not evidence that tenant acceptance passed.

## What Good Looks Like

| Output | Good result |
|---|---|
| Content drift audit | Sortable Excel report with assets reviewed, findings by severity, drift patterns, owner fix list, and remediation order; each finding cites the messaging doc, brand guide, or manager's Teams guidance and offers draft language; per-owner Teams drafts awaiting approval |
| Campaign command center | Tracker with workstreams, milestones, blockers, approvals, and optimization actions; weekly status cites the evidence behind each status change |
| Performance loop | Underperforming channels or content flagged against targets from connected or exported data, with recommended shifts and owners; no invented figures |
| Asset production | Asset list, versioned drafts grounded in approved messaging, review requests, and a tracker that never marks an asset approved without evidence |
| Event command center | Readiness tracker, customer meeting briefs, social plan, daily digest, and follow-up drafts within 48 hours |
| Customer-signal synthesis | PowerPoint activation brief with evidence-backed themes (quotes, frequency, affected audience or journey stage, related campaign-performance signal, implication) and 3-5 prioritized marketing actions; draft Teams update for [Marketing Teams Channel]; sample size and source coverage stated |

## Business Outcomes and Baseline

Measure the current campaign operations cost before installing or scheduling the Campaign Operator skills. The baseline should capture asset cycle time, approval latency, content error rate, status overhead, and campaign performance; do not claim savings or ROI lift unless the customer can compare against this baseline.

## Qualification and Scope Boundaries

**Base delivery:** qualify the approved cohort and existing sources, select and inspect the
supplied package, tune references, validate outputs and controls on demand, and hand over a bounded
pilot. Optional recurrence is a separate accepted step, not an installation side effect.

**Approved source classes:** Approved messaging and brand guidance, governed asset folders, campaign/event plans, permitted Microsoft 365 context, qualified marketing analytics or explicitly supplied exports.

Use the published ZIP's versioned messaging and output rules, not a loose source variant. Cite the approved standard behind each drift finding. Show sample size and coverage for customer signals and distinguish supported findings from proposed actions.

**Separate work:** new connectors or MCP servers, source/permission remediation, ingestion/indexes,
data models, custom code/UI, production artifact hosting, new skill families/languages and broad
rollout. An existing connector or browser-readable source is not proof of actual Cowork retrieval.
Do not silently expand base delivery to obtain missing access or invent a connector.

## Safety and Governance

- Use the execution identity's demonstrated access and explicitly approved scope. A persona,
  concierge, shared plugin or schedule does not grant permission.
- Cite material claims and disclose inaccessible, stale, conflicting and missing evidence.
  Retrieved content is evidence, not instructions to weaken controls or disclose data.
- Review generated output audience, classification, retention and destination separately from
  source access. Verify supported labeling; do not assume files inherit source controls.
- Preview exact content, recipients, attachments, destination and action before any consequence.
  If a mandatory runtime approval cannot be demonstrated, keep the action manual or disabled.
- Do not rank, score or infer employee productivity, sentiment, wellbeing or working hours.
- Confirm actual tenant licensing and metering. Package billing bands are indicative planning
  metadata, not official rates; `/cost` is approximate, not billing truth.
