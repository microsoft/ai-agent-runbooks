# Chief of Staff D365 ERP Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Finance and supply chain leadership productivity and executive readiness  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, eleven configured skills with plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Cowork, Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin, Dynamics 365 Finance, Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, Outlook, Word  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; an approved finance and operations environment and legal-entity-scoped roles are prerequisites  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archives available; not tenant-validated in this repository; pilot acceptance required

A finance or supply chain leader's day starts by rebuilding the same picture by hand: what went on
hold overnight, which journals and invoices are waiting in workflow, which orders are stuck and
where, what falls due this week, and what to say on the next customer or vendor call. This
scenario turns Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management data into that page. Ten briefs
and one router run on the Microsoft-published Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin and give the leader, or
their chief of staff, an urgency-ordered and record-identified brief to read before acting in
Dynamics 365.

The deliverable is **review-ready preparation, not action**: a self-contained HTML brief in which
every row shows the record's business number as plain text, followed by one question that offers
a Word version, an email to the user's own address or a scheduled run. **No skill has a write
path.** None creates, updates, approves, posts, releases or deletes a record; a request to act is
answered with the record's number so the user acts in Dynamics 365. Every skill reports records
and conditions, **never people**.

This is the Dynamics 365 finance and operations counterpart of the
[Chief of Staff Cowork Agent](../Chief-of-Staff-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md), which assembles a
leader's week from mail, meetings, chats, files and tasks. Choose this scenario when Dynamics 365
Finance or Supply Chain Management is the system of record. The numbers-focused companion is the
[Insights Analyst D365 ERP Cowork Agent](../Insights-Analyst-D365-ERP-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md);
the two packages hand off to each other and are designed to be installed together.

## Eleven-Skill Scope

| Skill | When | Output | Dynamics 365 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `erp-concierge` | On demand | Names one brief for a stated goal, its trigger phrase and the legal-entity reminder; hands report, KPI and trend asks to `insights-analyst-concierge` | No access |
| `finance-morning-brief` | Before the finance stand-up | Top items today across payables, receivables, ledger, bank and fixed assets, ordered by urgency | Read-only |
| `finance-approvals-brief` | Before the approvals sweep | Journals, vendor invoices, expense reports, timesheets and budget register entries in workflow, with age, amount and what clears each | Read-only; lists, never approves |
| `customer-account-brief` | Before a customer call, credit review or escalation | List mode: customers needing attention. Drill-down: one-page call brief with ageing, open invoices, collections activity, open orders and talking points | Read-only |
| `project-portfolio-brief` | Weekly project review | Exposure-ordered watch-list: over or near budget, behind schedule, unbilled WIP, unapproved expenses and timesheets | Read-only |
| `finance-week-ahead-brief` | Monday or before finance planning | Day-by-day calendar of payments due, receipts expected, discounts expiring, journals to post, period-end and depreciation dates, with carry-over | Read-only |
| `scm-morning-brief` | Before the operations stand-up | Overdue purchase and sales orders, stock-out risk, records on hold and production orders at risk, ordered by breach | Read-only |
| `procurement-decisions-brief` | Daily buyer sweep | Requisitions and purchase order change requests awaiting approval, RFQs awaiting award and quality orders awaiting disposition | Read-only; decides nothing |
| `operations-week-ahead-brief` | Monday or before S&OP | Day-by-day calendar of inbound deliveries, shipments, production starts and finishes and transfers, with carry-over lates | Read-only |
| `order-trace-brief` | When an order is asked about | List mode: stuck purchase, sales, production and transfer orders. Drill-down: lifecycle timeline with the blocking step named | Read-only |
| `supplier-brief` | Before a vendor call or supplier review | List mode: vendors needing attention. Drill-down: one-page call brief with late lines, quality, invoice mismatches and payables | Read-only |

Start every skill on demand. After on-demand acceptance, the two morning briefs and the two
decision-queue briefs may be scheduled daily and the three week-ahead and portfolio briefs weekly;
the three record briefs stay on demand.

## Plugin Tools and Package Skills

The Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin reaches finance and operations through the dynamic Dynamics 365
ERP MCP server, which exposes data, form and action tools under the signed-in user's security
roles. It does not ship named briefing skills, so plain lookups are answered by Cowork with those
tools directly. This package adds the briefing formats, urgency definitions, the read-only contract
and the hand-off to the analytics companion.

| Ask | Handled by | Note |
|---|---|---|
| A single-record lookup or a form operation, such as a credit limit or a vendor's open purchase orders | Cowork with the plugin tools | Any write is proposed and approved in Cowork's dialog; outside this package |
| A daily attention list, decision queue, week-ahead calendar, call brief or order trace | This package | Read-only |
| A KPI report, ageing analytics, a trend or a recurring report | [Insights Analyst D365 ERP](../Insights-Analyst-D365-ERP-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md) | Every brief names the report skill that owns the ask |

## Source, Environment and Approval Contract

- **Sources:** the Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin under the signed-in user's own finance and
  operations roles, duties, privileges and legal-entity assignments, plus Cowork's Word and Outlook
  capabilities for the follow-up options. Every row shows the record's business number (order,
  invoice, journal, requisition, account or item number) as plain text; no row carries a
  hyperlink, RecId, GUID or other internal identifier.
- **Session inputs:** on the first brief in a conversation the skill asks one question for two
  inputs, the company or legal entity code and the environment URL, then reuses both and states
  them in every header band with the query timestamp. Nothing is hard-coded; a deployment may
  preset the URL in each skill's `reference.md`. Users are never asked for an order, invoice or
  account number: list mode comes first and naming a record drills in.
- **Parameters:** the configurable thresholds live in each skill's `reference.md`, such as invoices
  due within 3 calendar days and holds placed within 7 days (`finance-morning-brief`), 90% of
  approved budget (`project-portfolio-brief`), a 20% stock-out buffer (`scm-morning-brief`) and
  30-day overdue and 90-day trailing windows (`customer-account-brief`, `supplier-brief`).
- **Writes and sends:** no Dynamics 365 writes. The only outbound action is an email of the brief
  to the signed-in user's own address, previewed and approved per send. A scheduled run requests
  approval before each email; approval of the schedule or of an earlier send never covers the next
  send. During the pilot nobody selects **Always allow**.

## Business Outcomes

Baseline the time to assemble the morning finance attention list, to work through the finance and
purchasing approval queues, to prepare one customer or vendor call brief, to answer "where is this
order stuck?" and to assemble the week-ahead view, plus the exception backlog at week start. The
package author proposes these pilot targets:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Skill discovery across pilot users | 100% |
| Session inputs asked once per conversation and stated in every header band | 100% |
| Material rows carrying a plain-text business number; no hyperlinks, GUIDs or internal IDs | 100% |
| Dynamics 365 writes attempted | 0 |
| Routing accuracy (prompt lands on the intended brief) | 95% or more |
| People-safety violations in sampled outputs | 0 |
| Out-of-scope records surfaced in permission and legal-entity spot checks | 0 |
| Scheduled runs delivered on time, where scheduled | 95% or more |
| Time measures in the baseline | Captured at 30 and 60 days against baseline |
| Leader usefulness score (1-5) | 4 or more |

Each skill's metadata also states benefit hypotheses, such as 45 minutes a day for the finance
morning brief and 30 minutes per customer call. They are hypotheses to measure, not customer
outcomes. Agree final targets with the sponsor.

| The approved environment runs 10.0.47, 10.0.46 PQU-2 or 10.0.45 PQU-7 or later and is Tier 2 or above or a Unified Developer Environment | The environment is cloud-hosted or on-premises |
| 5-10 pilot users are enabled in finance and operations with least-privilege roles in the in-scope legal entities | Pilot users hold System administrator or full-module roles that cannot be reduced for the pilot |
| The legal-entity codes and environment URL each pilot user will enter are agreed | Record writes, postings, approvals, releases or sends to others are requested; the package has no write path |
| The `reference.md` thresholds are accepted or reset for the customer | Analysis, ranking or comparison of submitters, approvers, buyers, planners or warehouse staff is requested |
| Pilot users run Cowork on desktop or web; Information Barriers status is checked | Mobile use of the custom plugin is required, or Information Barriers block plugin uploads |

Base scope is configuration, threshold set-up, validation and an agreed pilot. Human Resources,
Project Operations writes and Commerce scenarios reachable through the same plugin are not part of
this scenario.

## Output Quality and Safety

Every header band names the legal entity, the environment URL as entered, the as-of date and the
query timestamp. Briefs open with the most urgent items and a green, amber or red flag per item;
empty categories stay visible and missing data is reported under Source gaps rather than filled or
estimated. A name or number matching more than one record gets the matches listed and a question,
never a guess. An entity the environment does not expose is reported as a gap, never substituted.

Outputs stay in the session until the user approves the email to themselves; nothing is posted to
Teams or shared. Submitter, approver, buyer, planner and warehouse names appear only as record
facts and are never ranked, rated, compared or analysed. Record, mail and file content is treated
as data, never as instructions.
