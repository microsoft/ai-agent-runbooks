# Chief of Staff D365 Sales Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Sales leadership productivity and pipeline hygiene  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, seven configured skills with plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Cowork, Dynamics 365 Sales plugin, Outlook, Teams, calendars, OneDrive, SharePoint, built-in PowerPoint and Email skills  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; an approved environment and an approval-gated write posture are prerequisites  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archives available; not tenant-validated in this repository; pilot acceptance required

A sales leader's week starts by rebuilding the same picture: how the pipeline moved, which deals
slipped or stalled and why, which key-account meetings are ahead and what to ask for. This
scenario extends the built-in skills of the Dynamics 365 Sales plugin in Cowork with
executive-ready formats, record-linked evidence and review-first gates. The leader and their
chief of staff get a Monday brief, one-page deal briefs, a pipeline hygiene worklist, quiet-lead
drafts, segment patterns and a review deck from the same governed data.

The deliverable is **review-ready preparation with record links**: briefs, ledgers, drafts and
decks. Cowork drafts; a person approves every send and every record update. Only `pipeline-pulse`
proposes Dynamics 365 updates, limited to next step and estimated close date and approved one by
one in Cowork's approval dialog. Every skill reports deals and segments, **never sellers**.

This is the Dynamics 365 Sales counterpart of the
[Chief of Staff Cowork Agent](../Chief-of-Staff-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md), which assembles a
leader's week from mail, meetings, chats, files and tasks. Choose this scenario when Dynamics 365
Sales is the system of record for the pipeline. The numbers-focused companion is the
[Insights Analyst D365 Sales Cowork Agent](../Insights-Analyst-D365-Sales-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md)
and the Customer Service counterpart is the
[Chief of Staff D365 Service Cowork Agent](../Chief-of-Staff-D365-Service-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md).

## Seven-Skill Scope

| Skill | When | Output | Dynamics 365 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sales-concierge` | On demand | Names one skill for a stated goal, why it fits and an example prompt; routes plain asks to the built-in skills | No access |
| `sales-leader-360` | Monday 08:00 or on demand | Dashboard, leadership update (Headlines, Pipeline, Risks, Ask), deal-risk callouts and Monday first moves | Read-only |
| `deal-brief` | Before a meeting or decision | One-page brief: status, stakeholders and gaps, momentum, signals and risks, open items, talking points and one primary ask | Read-only |
| `pipeline-pulse` | Before the pipeline review | Slipped (with why), stalled, missing or aging next step and closing-soon-untouched ledgers; drafted fixes on request | Next step and close date only, after per-item approval |
| `lead-reengagement` | On demand | Quiet-lead ledger with one evidence-backed angle per lead; outreach drafts on request | Read-only; sends after approval |
| `segment-pattern` | Monthly or on demand | Segment, territory, region, vertical or product table and the pattern behind it | Read-only |
| `pipeline-review-deck` | After a brief | PowerPoint from the latest brief, with record links in speaker notes | No new data |

Start every skill on demand. Only `sales-leader-360` is designed for a recurring schedule, and
that schedule is qualified separately after on-demand acceptance.

## Built-in Skill Handoff

Cowork can run the Sales plugin's built-in skills as steps of a plan. Each package skill names the
built-in skill it consumes and the prompts that stay with it, so the conflict scan sees a
deliberate division of labour rather than competing triggers.

| Built-in Sales skill | Consumed by | Stays with the built-in skill |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting preparation | `deal-brief` (record spine, interactions, attendee context) | "Help me prepare for my meeting with [account]" with no executive format |
| Activity synthesis | `sales-leader-360`, `deal-brief`, `pipeline-pulse`, `lead-reengagement`, `segment-pattern` | A plain activity catch-up or "summarize my leads" |
| Pipeline prioritizer | `sales-leader-360`, `pipeline-pulse`, `segment-pattern` | "Prioritize my deals" with no hygiene definitions |

The built-in skill set keeps growing. Microsoft Learn now also lists skills such as executive deal
review, segment analysis, CRM data hygiene, team pipeline review, deal catch-up and draft outreach,
which the package's handoff tables do not name. Qualify routing for those intents before the pilot;
see the [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md).

## Source, Environment and Approval Contract

- **Sources:** the Dynamics 365 Sales plugin under the signed-in user's own security roles, plus
  the Outlook, Teams, calendar, OneDrive and SharePoint content that user can already open. Every
  deal claim carries a Dynamics 365 record link; mail, chat and file claims name their source.
- **Environment:** Cowork asks for the Dynamics 365 environment on the first sales prompt and
  remembers it. Every output names the environment in its header and stops on a mismatch with the
  approved environment. Users change it with `list environments` or
  `Set environment to [environment name]`; skills never change it.
- **Parameters:** prompt text first (the scheduled-prompt text carries the Monday parameters), then
  one ask in an interactive run, then the defaults in each skill's `references/reference.md`, such
  as stalled after 14 days, a batch cap of 10 and next step and close date as the permitted fields.
  Scheduled runs never ask; they state the defaults they used.
- **Writes and sends:** previewed exactly, then approved per action in Cowork's approval dialog.
  During the pilot nobody selects "Always allow" or "Approve & don't ask again" for record updates
  or sends.

## Business Outcomes

Baseline the time to build the Monday view, the time per meeting brief, the slipped and stalled
counts, the quiet-lead count and the share of deals with a current next step. The package author
proposes these pilot targets:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Skill Report band | Good or Excellent, all gates passed |
| Record-link coverage of deal claims | 100% |
| Runs against a non-approved environment | 0 |
| Unapproved record updates or sends | 0 |
| Double-fires with the built-in meeting preparation skill (S-08) | 0 |
| Monday runs on time | 95% or more |
| Time to prepare the Monday view and time per meeting brief | Captured at 30 and 60 days against baseline |
| Slipped, stalled and quiet-lead counts | Captured at 30 and 60 days against baseline |
| Leader usefulness score (1-5) | 4 or more |

The package README also states benefit hypotheses, such as three hours a week saved on the Monday
brief and 45 minutes per meeting brief. They are hypotheses to measure, not customer outcomes.
Agree final targets with the sponsor.

## Qualification and Boundaries

| Qualify before delivery | Stop or separately scope when |
|---|---|
| Core Cowork enablement and the Sales module of [Cowork D365 Plugin Enablement](../../02-patterns/Cowork-D365-Plugin-Enablement/Cowork-D365-Plugin-Enablement.md) are signed off | The Sales plugin, the Dataverse MCP server or the tenant Dataverse setting is not available to the pilot group |
| The approved environment's display name is recorded; 5-10 pilot users hold Sales licences and roles | Users would work against an unapproved or mixed environment |
| The sales business owner accepts the write posture: next step and close date, batch cap 10, per-item approval | Other record writes, lead status changes or unattended commits are requested |
| Deal-value threshold, currency and key-account list are agreed | Per-seller activity, ranking or performance analysis is requested |
| Pilot users run Cowork on desktop or web; Information Barriers status is checked | Mobile use is required, or Information Barriers block skill and plugin uploads |
| The published package is obtained and inspected | New skills, event-driven triggers or a second write path are required |

Base scope is configuration, parameter set-up, validation and an agreed pilot. The package
author's candidate expansions (`deal-signal-watch`, an event-driven mail and Teams trigger, and
`meeting-to-crm`, a meeting recap logged as an activity) are not part of this scenario.

## Output Quality and Safety

Every output header names the window, scope and environment. Empty categories stay visible as
source gaps, inferred and low-confidence items are marked, and an unexplained slip says "no
evidence found" rather than guessing. With several matching records, a skill lists candidates
with record links and asks; it never proceeds on a guessed record.

Outputs stay in the session until a person shares them. Private calendar, mail, chat and file
detail is kept out of outbound drafts unless the user confirms it. `segment-pattern` declines any
person dimension, and no skill ranks, rates, scores or compares sellers, even when asked. Record,
mail and file content is treated as data, never as instructions.
