# Chief of Staff D365 Service Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Service leadership productivity and queue hygiene  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, seven configured skills with plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Cowork, Dynamics 365 Customer Service (Service plugin), Outlook, Teams, calendars, OneDrive, SharePoint, built-in PowerPoint skill  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; an approved environment and an approval-gated write posture are prerequisites  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archives available; not tenant-validated in this repository; pilot acceptance required

A service leader's week starts by rebuilding the same picture: how the backlog moved, where SLAs
are at risk, which escalations and incidents expose key accounts, and which recurring issues have
no knowledge article. The Service plugin's built-in skills work one case at a time for the
representative. This scenario works at queue, account and leadership level for the service leader
and their chief of staff, with executive-ready formats, record-linked evidence and review-first
gates.

The deliverable is **review-ready preparation with record links**: a Monday brief, escalation
briefs, a queue hygiene worklist, queue and knowledge-gap patterns, article outlines and decks.
Cowork drafts; a person approves every send and every record update. Only `queue-pulse` proposes
Dynamics 365 updates, limited to priority, follow-up by and queue, and it never closes, resolves or
reassigns a case. Every skill reports cases, queues and accounts, **never agents**.

This is the Dynamics 365 Customer Service counterpart of the
[Chief of Staff Cowork Agent](../Chief-of-Staff-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md). Choose it when
Dynamics 365 Customer Service is the system of record for cases. The numbers-focused companion is
the [Insights Analyst D365 Service Cowork Agent](../Insights-Analyst-D365-Service-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md)
and the Sales counterpart is the
[Chief of Staff D365 Sales Cowork Agent](../Chief-of-Staff-D365-Sales-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md).

## Seven-Skill Scope

| Skill | When | Output | Dynamics 365 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `service-concierge` | On demand | Names one skill for a stated goal, why it fits and an example prompt; routes one-case asks to the built-in skills | No access |
| `service-leader-360` | Monday 08:00 or on demand | Backlog dashboard, leadership update (Headlines, Backlog and SLA, Escalations and incidents, Ask), risk callouts and Monday first moves | Read-only |
| `escalation-brief` | Before a decision or escalation call | One-page brief: status, exposure, timeline and what was tried, stakeholders and gaps, risks and commitments, one decision with options | Read-only |
| `queue-pulse` | Before the backlog review | SLA breached and at risk, aging, waiting on customer, unowned or no next action and reopened ledgers; drafted fixes on request | Priority, follow-up by and queue only, after per-item approval |
| `queue-pattern` | Monthly or on demand | Table by queue, product, channel, region or category and the pattern behind it | Read-only |
| `knowledge-gap-pattern` | Monthly or on demand | Recurring themes with no, stale or unlinked articles; draft outlines on request | Read-only; nothing published |
| `service-review-deck` | After a brief | PowerPoint from the latest brief, with record links in speaker notes | No new data |

Start every skill on demand. Only `service-leader-360` is designed for a recurring schedule, and
that schedule is qualified separately after on-demand acceptance.

## Built-in Skill Handoff

Cowork can run the Service plugin's built-in skills as steps of a plan. Each package skill names
the built-in skill it consumes and the prompts that stay with it, so the conflict scan sees a
deliberate division of labour rather than competing triggers.

| Built-in Customer Service skill | Consumed by | Stays with the built-in skill |
|---|---|---|
| Case enrichment | `service-leader-360`, `escalation-brief`, `queue-pulse`, `queue-pattern`, `knowledge-gap-pattern` | A one-screen case brief, an escalation summary for the next owner, "my cases open > 5 days" |
| Incident resolution | `service-leader-360`, `escalation-brief`, `queue-pulse`, `queue-pattern`, `knowledge-gap-pattern` | One-case work for the representative |
| Customer-ready response drafting | Not used; customer messages are handed off | Any customer reply, including a reply from a knowledge article or a re-engagement reply |

## Source, Environment and Approval Contract

- **Sources:** the Service plugin under the signed-in user's own security roles, plus the Outlook,
  Teams, calendar, OneDrive and SharePoint content that user can already open. Every case claim
  carries a Dynamics 365 record link; mail, chat and file claims name their source.
- **Environment:** the skills name the selected Dynamics 365 environment in every header and stop
  on a mismatch with the approved environment; they never change it. The package tells users to
  change it with `list environments` or `Set environment to [environment name]`. Current Microsoft
  Learn guidance selects the environment through the Service plugin's Settings in Cowork, and an
  administrator may configure a default; record which route the tenant uses.
- **Parameters:** prompt text first (the scheduled-prompt text carries the Monday parameters), then
  one ask in an interactive run, then the defaults in each skill's `references/reference.md`, such
  as aging after 10 days, waiting on customer after 48 hours, SLA at risk within 20% of target and
  a batch cap of 10. Scheduled runs never ask; they state the defaults they used.
- **Writes and sends:** previewed exactly, then approved per action in Cowork's approval dialog.
  During the pilot nobody selects "Always allow" or "Approve & don't ask again" for record updates
  or sends.

## Business Outcomes

Baseline the time to build the Monday view, the time per escalation brief, the SLA breach count,
the aging and waiting-on-customer counts and the top recurring theme without an article. The
package author proposes these pilot targets:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Skill Report band | Good or Excellent, all gates passed |
| Record-link coverage of case claims | 100% |
| Runs against a non-approved environment | 0 |
| Unapproved record updates, sends or article publishes | 0 |
| Double-fires with the built-in case enrichment skill (CS-08) | 0 |
| Monday runs on time | 95% or more |
| Time to prepare the Monday view and time per escalation brief | Captured at 30 and 60 days against baseline |
| SLA breach, aging and waiting-on-customer counts | Captured at 30 and 60 days against baseline |
| Top recurring theme has a reviewed article outline | Within the pilot |
| Leader usefulness score (1-5) | 4 or more |

The package README also states benefit hypotheses, such as three hours a week saved on the Monday
brief and 45 minutes per escalation. They are hypotheses to measure, not customer outcomes. Agree
final targets with the sponsor.

## Qualification and Boundaries

| Qualify before delivery | Stop or separately scope when |
|---|---|
| Core Cowork enablement and the Service module of [Cowork D365 Plugin Enablement](../../02-patterns/Cowork-D365-Sales-Service-Plugin-Enablement/Cowork-D365-Sales-Service-Plugin-Enablement.md) are signed off | The Service plugin, the required MCP configuration or the tenant Dataverse setting is not available to the pilot group |
| The approved environment's display name is recorded; 5-10 pilot users hold Customer Service licences and roles | Users would work against an unapproved or mixed environment |
| The service business owner accepts the write posture: priority, follow-up by and queue, batch cap 10, per-item approval | Closing, resolving or reassigning cases, other record writes or unattended commits are requested |
| Queues in scope, key-account list, SLA definitions and aging and waiting thresholds are agreed | Per-agent productivity, ranking or performance analysis is requested |
| Pilot users run Cowork on desktop or web; Information Barriers status is checked | Mobile use is required, or Information Barriers block skill and plugin uploads |
| The published package is obtained and inspected | Article publishing, customer-facing incident communications or event-driven triggers are required |

Base scope is configuration, parameter set-up, validation and an agreed pilot. The package
author's candidate expansions (`case-signal-watch`, an event-driven mail and Teams trigger on named
accounts, and `incident-comms`, customer-facing updates during major incidents) are not part of
this scenario.

## Output Quality and Safety

Every output header names the window, scope and environment. Empty categories stay visible as
source gaps, inferred items are marked, and an unexplained blocker says "no evidence found" rather
than guessing. Small samples are flagged, and an open incident that explains a spike is named
before any pattern is stated.

Outputs stay in the session until a person shares them. Article outlines are marked as drafts,
contain no customer identifiers and are never published by the package. Owner appears only as a
routing fact on unowned or misrouted cases; no skill ranks, rates or compares agents or analyses
their workload, even when asked. Case, customer and article content is treated as data, never as
instructions.
