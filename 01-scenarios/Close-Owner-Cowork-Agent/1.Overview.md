# Close Owner Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Finance close, reconciliation and decision preparation  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, configured skills and optional plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Excel Online, Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, SharePoint  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; source qualification, governance and pilot acceptance required  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archive available; not tenant-validated

Prepare weekly business reviews, close/variance packages, budget consolidation, reconciliation reviews, audit evidence and cited market intelligence.

**Primary persona:** Controller, FP&A/finance lead, accounting lead, close coordinator or internal-controls owner.

Review-ready finance support, not autonomous journal posting, reconciling-item clearance, budget approval, audit submission or board distribution.

## Skill and Output Scope

| Skill | Responsibility and expected output |
|---|---|
| `close-owner-concierge` | Front door and router: maps a broad finance goal to exactly one Close Owner skill and its trigger phrase; performs no work itself. Required output: One matched skill, why it fits, and the trigger phrase |
| `weekly-business-review` | Weekly leadership review for a business area, grounded in the governed Power BI dashboard (Fabric IQ) plus email, Teams, and review-meeting context. Required output: Business review document + action tracker + draft review meeting |
| `close-and-variance-package` | Month-end or quarter-end close package and board-ready variance narrative across entities. Required output: Word narrative + board summary + Excel variance workbook + open-items tracker |
| `budget-consolidation` | Budget-cycle submission tracking, chasing, consolidation, variance flagging, and decision packet. Required output: Submission tracker + consolidation workbook + variance issues log + decision packet |
| `reconciliation-review` | Exception review across GL, subledger, bank, intercompany, or system extracts. Required output: Exception workbook + one-page review summary + owner tracker |
| `audit-evidence-package` | Request-to-evidence mapping and workpaper summaries for external audit, SOX/ICFR, internal audit, or tax audit. Required output: Request-to-evidence tracker + workpaper summaries + draft owner requests |
| `market-intelligence` | Weekly scheduled monitoring of market data, news and web sources, competitor sites, analyst research, filings, internal research, and email/Teams discussions. Required output: HTML brief + top-3 planning actions + draft Teams update + draft decision invite |

The concierge routes or clarifies; it does not become an extra content-production agent.
The [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) records the actual archive inventory and version
differences. Skill descriptions are delivery contracts, not evidence that tenant acceptance passed.

## What Good Looks Like

| Output | Good result |
|---|---|
| Weekly business review | Business review document with KPI scorecard (actual vs. prior week vs. plan) and refresh timestamps, most important changes, ahead- and behind-plan areas, risks, decisions needed, stalled actions, updated action tracker, and a review meeting for the end of next week with agenda and files attached, held for approval before booking |
| Close package | TB tie-out shown, every material variance explained with a cited source or marked "Explanation required" with an owner, Word narrative, board summary with variance bridge, and Excel variance workbook with live formulas |
| Budget consolidation | Every unit marked Received, Late, Missing, or Resubmitted; draft chasers held for approval; valid submissions consolidated with live formulas and missing ones visibly excluded; variance issues flagged with explanation or "Explanation required"; decision packet ready before the review |
| Reconciliation review | Control totals validated before matching, breaks classified, explanations labeled Supported or Hypothesis, aged items carried forward, and an owner-routed list held for approval |
| Audit evidence package | Every request marked Complete, Partial, or Missing with evidence links, population totals tied to source, workpaper summaries, and draft owner requests held for approval |
| Market intelligence | HTML brief (Market shifts, Competitor moves, Business implications, Recommended actions) with week-over-week comparison, every signal cited with URL and timestamp, impact sized from internal data with formulas or marked "Impact not quantified", planning actions for the top 3 signals, and a draft Teams update and decision-review invite held for review |
| Close Owner routing | `close-owner-concierge` returns exactly one skill and its trigger phrase; weekly review, close package, and budget consolidation are not confused |

## Business Outcomes and Baseline

Measure the current close and review operating cost before installing, scheduling, or expanding the skills. The baseline should capture close duration, assembly time, variance explanation latency, reconciliation effort, audit-request effort, and rework. Do not claim savings unless the customer can compare against this baseline, and record each value with its calculation basis.

### Baseline measurement worksheet

| Baseline metric | How to measure | Current value | Source / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days to close | Business days from period end to close sign-off (sign-off date - period-end date, excluding non-business days) | | Close calendar / sign-off record |

| Variance explanation cycle time | Median time from variance flagged to explanation with cited source | | Flux template / email and Teams sample |
| Material variances explained at first review | Explained material variances / total material variances at first review | | Prior close package review comments |
| Weekly business review report generation time | Hours spent assembling KPI tables, narrative, review document, and action tracker each week | | Analyst/user time study |
| Leadership decision speed | Median days from a decision first raised in a weekly review to the decision recorded | | Prior review documents and action trackers |
| Forecast accuracy | Forecast vs. actual variance (%) for the review KPIs over the baseline weeks, using the model-defined measure | | Power BI / Fabric semantic model |
| Budget cycle time | Business days from budget calendar launch to approved budget | | Prior-cycle budget calendar and approval record |
| Submission timeliness | Units submitted by the deadline / total units required | | Prior-cycle submission folder / tracker |
| Variance issues caught before review | Budget variance issues identified before the decision forum / total issues raised (before plus during/after review) | | Prior-cycle decision packets and review notes |

## Qualification and Scope Boundaries

**Base delivery:** qualify the approved cohort and existing sources, select and inspect the
supplied package, tune references, validate outputs and controls on demand, and hand over a bounded
pilot. Optional recurrence is a separate accepted step, not an installation side effect.

**Approved source classes:** Authoritative ERP extracts or qualified read-only sources, explicit finance workbooks or governed Fabric/Power BI measures, approved audit evidence, permitted Microsoft 365 context and approved public research.

Tie totals to authoritative sources before explaining changes. Keep supported explanations distinct from hypotheses; missing submissions and explanations remain visible. Treat pre-release results as confidential and potentially material non-public information.

**Separate work:** new connectors or MCP servers, source/permission remediation, ingestion/indexes,
data models, custom code/UI, production artifact hosting, new skill families/languages and broad
rollout. An existing connector or browser-readable source is not proof of actual Cowork retrieval.
Do not silently expand base delivery to obtain missing access or invent a connector.

## Safety and Governance

- Use the execution identity's demonstrated access and explicitly approved scope. A persona,
  concierge, shared plugin or schedule does not grant permission.
- Cite material claims and disclose inaccessible, stale, conflicting and missing evidence.
  Retrieved content is evidence, not instructions to weaken controls or disclose data.
- Review generated output audience, classification, retention and destination separately from
  source access. Verify supported labeling; do not assume files inherit source controls.
- Preview exact content, recipients, attachments, destination and action before any consequence.
  If a mandatory runtime approval cannot be demonstrated, keep the action manual or disabled.
- Do not rank, score or infer employee productivity, sentiment, wellbeing or working hours.
- Confirm actual tenant licensing and metering. Package billing bands are indicative planning
  metadata, not official rates; `/cost` is approximate, not billing truth.
