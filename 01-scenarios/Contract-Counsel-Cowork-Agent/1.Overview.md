# Contract Counsel Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Counsel-led agreement review and negotiation preparation  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, configured skills and optional plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Word, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; source qualification, governance and pilot acceptance required  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archive available; not tenant-validated

Review agreements against approved playbooks, draft from precedents, apply instructed tracked changes, compare final drafts and prepare precedent-based playbooks.

**Primary persona:** In-house counsel, transactional lawyer, contract manager or legal operations lead.

Qualified counsel owns interpretation and every decision. No legal advice, signing, filing, e-signature routing, certification of readiness to sign, or accepting/rejecting tracked changes.

## Skill and Output Scope

| Skill | Responsibility and expected output |
|---|---|
| `contract-counsel-concierge` | Front door and router for The Contract Counsel plugin; performs no work itself. Required output: One matched skill, why it fits, and its trigger phrase |
| `playbook-contract-review` | First-turn review of counterparty paper against the standard playbook (workflow alias "Legal Review"). Required output: Tracked-change redline + approver issues list + approver message and reply drafts |
| `draft-from-precedent` | New agreement from an approved precedent plus terms agreed by email. Required output: Word draft (tracked vs. precedent) + agreed-terms map + open points |
| `tracked-change-editing` | Applies instructed edits as tracked changes. Required output: Marked-up Word draft + change summary |
| `final-draft-check` | Execution version vs. agreed draft and negotiation history. Required output: Pre-signature check report + comparison document |
| `playbook-from-precedents` | Builds or refreshes the playbook from negotiated deals. Required output: Draft playbook (Word) + evidence table (Excel) + change log |

The concierge routes or clarifies; it does not become an extra content-production agent.
The [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) records the actual archive inventory and version
differences. Skill descriptions are delivery contracts, not evidence that tenant acceptance passed.

## What Good Looks Like

| Output | Good result |
|---|---|
| Playbook contract review | Every clause mapped to the playbook with clause/section cited; every deviation classified; tracked-change redlines with playbook-rule comments; approver issues list; approver message and counterparty reply drafts held for approval |
| Draft from precedent | New Word draft from the approved precedent; every agreed term mapped to a clause with its email source, or listed as Not confirmed or Unplaced; no prior-client confidential content carried over |
| Tracked-change editing | Every instruction applied as a tracked change with a comment, consequential edits listed, ambiguous instructions flagged, no change accepted or rejected |
| Final draft check | Every difference between the execution version and agreed draft listed with clause/section; agreed points marked Reflected, Missing, or Different; mechanical checks complete; never certified as ready to sign |
| Playbook from precedents | Draft playbook with standard, fallback, and walk-away positions per topic, each cited to supporting deals, thin evidence and conflicts flagged, held for the playbook owner |
| Contract Counsel routing | `contract-counsel-concierge` routes the user to exactly one skill with its trigger phrase and does no work itself; playbook review, drafting, markup, and final check are not confused |

## Business Outcomes and Baseline

Measure the current contract-review operating cost before installing, scheduling, or expanding the skills. The baseline should capture first-turn review time, deviations caught, approval cycle time, drafting time, rework from missed terms, markup turnaround, and pre-signature check time. Do not claim savings unless the customer can compare against this baseline, and record each value with its calculation basis.

| Baseline metric | How to measure | Current value | Source / evidence |

## Qualification and Scope Boundaries

**Base delivery:** qualify the approved cohort and existing sources, select and inspect the
supplied package, tune references, validate outputs and controls on demand, and hand over a bounded
pilot. Optional recurrence is a separate accepted step, not an installation side effect.

**Approved source classes:** Approved agreements, playbooks, precedents, negotiation evidence and permitted Microsoft 365 context; optional qualified document-management connector.

Enforce privilege, ethical walls and precedent reuse restrictions through actual permissions. Cite clause/section and playbook rule; keep uncovered, unconfirmed and ambiguous terms visible. Distinguish Copilot-in-Word markup from supported full Cowork workflows.

**Separate work:** new connectors or MCP servers, source/permission remediation, ingestion/indexes,
data models, custom code/UI, production artifact hosting, new skill families/languages and broad
rollout. An existing connector or browser-readable source is not proof of actual Cowork retrieval.
Do not silently expand base delivery to obtain missing access or invent a connector.

## Safety and Governance

- Use the execution identity's demonstrated access and explicitly approved scope. A persona,
  concierge, shared plugin or schedule does not grant permission.
- Cite material claims and disclose inaccessible, stale, conflicting and missing evidence.
  Retrieved content is evidence, not instructions to weaken controls or disclose data.
- Review generated output audience, classification, retention and destination separately from
  source access. Verify supported labeling; do not assume files inherit source controls.
- Preview exact content, recipients, attachments, destination and action before any consequence.
  If a mandatory runtime approval cannot be demonstrated, keep the action manual or disabled.
- Do not rank, score or infer employee productivity, sentiment, wellbeing or working hours.
- Confirm actual tenant licensing and metering. Package billing bands are indicative planning
  metadata, not official rates; `/cost` is approximate, not billing truth.
