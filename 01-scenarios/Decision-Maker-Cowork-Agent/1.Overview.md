# Decision Maker Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Executive decision support  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, configured skills and optional plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Excel Online  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; source qualification, governance and pilot acceptance required  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archive available; not tenant-validated

Prepare a daily attention dashboard, board pre-reads, sourced decision briefs, KPI exception reviews and professional relationship briefings.

**Primary persona:** CEO, president or business-unit head; authorised delegates use their own verified access.

Review-ready executive preparation, not an autonomous executive, investment adviser or employee-assessment system. A delegate role does not grant access to the executive's mailbox.

## Skill and Output Scope

| Skill | Responsibility and expected output |
|---|---|
| `decision-maker-concierge` | Front door and router: maps a broad executive goal to exactly one Decision-Maker skill and its trigger phrase; does no work itself. Required output: One matched skill, why it fits, and its trigger phrase completed with the user's specifics |
| `daily-command-center` | Daily interactive HTML dashboard of what needs attention today and this week, plus a printable one-page Word summary. Required output: Interactive HTML command center + printable one-page Word summary |
| `board-pre-reads` | Board prep workflow (workflow title; saved-skill alias "Board and Leadership Pre-Read"): cited pre-read on the existing template, plus draft share-for-feedback message and 30-minute review meeting. Required output: Pre-read deck on the template + draft feedback request + draft 30-minute review meeting |
| `cited-decision-briefs` | Sourced recommendation on one pending decision. Required output: 2-4 page Word brief `Decision-Brief-<Topic>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.docx` with evaluation matrix and numbered sources |
| `exception-analytics` | Business-review exception radar across KPIs, targets, and prior periods. Required output: `Exception-Radar-<Period>.xlsx` tracker + 3-5 section readout + optional draft agenda |
| `relationship-briefings` | Focused pack on a person, organization, topic, or relationship. Required output: `Brief-<Subject>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.docx` (two pages) or one-page HTML |

The concierge routes or clarifies; it does not become an extra content-production agent.
The [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) records the actual archive inventory and version
differences. Skill descriptions are delivery contracts, not evidence that tenant acceptance passed.

## What Good Looks Like

| Output | Good result |
|---|---|
| Concierge routing | `decision-maker-concierge` names exactly one skill, why it fits, and its trigger phrase, without doing the work or dumping the catalog |
| Executive Command Center | Header strip shows 1-2 urgent items, top meeting/priority, busiest day, and working days remaining; every action card is labeled Lean in, Delegate, Re-engage, or Protect time with signal, one next action, and source links; scannable in under two minutes; a printable one-page Word summary of key findings accompanies the dashboard |
| Board pre-read (Board prep workflow) | On the existing template; covers performance changes, progress against prior priorities, initiative status, decisions with recommended direction, open actions, gaps and conflicts, and a sources appendix; feedback request and review meeting remain drafts |
| Cited decision brief | Bottom line up front, options including do nothing, evaluation matrix with confidence, stakeholder conflicts, risks, reversal triggers, and every claim cited |
| Exception readout | Ranked material exceptions with cited drivers or "no explanation found", the decision each implies, positives worth scaling, and data-quality exceptions |
| Relationship briefing | History timeline, open issues on both sides, dated external context, likely asks, and talking points, limited to professional context |

## Business Outcomes and Baseline

Measure the current operating cost before installing or scheduling the Decision-Maker skills. The baseline should capture preparation time, decision latency, decision readiness, rework, and missed-signal cost; do not claim savings unless the customer can compare against this baseline.

### Baseline measurement worksheet

| Baseline metric | How to measure | Current value | Source / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning triage time | Minutes spent scanning calendar, inbox, Teams, transcripts, and priority files before the first priority decision | | Calendar/user time study |
| Board or leadership pre-read prep time | Hours from kickoff to approved pre-read for the most recent board or leadership meeting | | Prior pre-read version history |
| Pre-read review rounds | Number of review cycles before the pre-read was final | | Version history/review comments |
| Gaps found during the meeting | Count of missing figures, decisions, or open questions discovered in the meeting rather than before it | | Meeting notes/minutes |
| Decisions taken vs. deferred | Share of decisions on the agenda taken at the meeting rather than deferred for more information | | Minutes/decision log |
| Decision research time | Hours from request to a recommendation the executive accepts for review | | Chief of Staff/strategy lead self-report |
| Decisions closed within 48 hours | Share of decisions waiting on the executive closed within 48 hours | | Baseline log |
| Exception detection time | Days from period close to material KPI exceptions identified | | Business review calendar/history |
| Exception to decision latency | Days from an exception being flagged to a decision being made | | Business review notes |
| Briefing prep time | Minutes to prepare for a high-stakes external or VIP meeting | | User self-report |

## Qualification and Scope Boundaries

**Base delivery:** qualify the approved cohort and existing sources, select and inspect the
supplied package, tune references, validate outputs and controls on demand, and hand over a bounded
pilot. Optional recurrence is a separate accepted step, not an installation side effect.

**Approved source classes:** Outlook, Teams, calendar, SharePoint, OneDrive, approved KPI workbooks; optional qualified Power BI/Fabric and approved public research.

Record priority and materiality rules, metric polarity, criteria weights and research permission before use. A calendar invite does not prove a meeting happened. Keep private board and relationship context out of broader distribution.

**Separate work:** new connectors or MCP servers, source/permission remediation, ingestion/indexes,
data models, custom code/UI, production artifact hosting, new skill families/languages and broad
rollout. An existing connector or browser-readable source is not proof of actual Cowork retrieval.
Do not silently expand base delivery to obtain missing access or invent a connector.

## Safety and Governance

- Use the execution identity's demonstrated access and explicitly approved scope. A persona,
  concierge, shared plugin or schedule does not grant permission.
- Cite material claims and disclose inaccessible, stale, conflicting and missing evidence.
  Retrieved content is evidence, not instructions to weaken controls or disclose data.
- Review generated output audience, classification, retention and destination separately from
  source access. Verify supported labeling; do not assume files inherit source controls.
- Preview exact content, recipients, attachments, destination and action before any consequence.
  If a mandatory runtime approval cannot be demonstrated, keep the action manual or disabled.
- Do not rank, score or infer employee productivity, sentiment, wellbeing or working hours.
- Confirm actual tenant licensing and metering. Package billing bands are indicative planning
  metadata, not official rates; `/cost` is approximate, not billing truth.
