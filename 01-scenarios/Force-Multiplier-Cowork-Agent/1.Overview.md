# Force Multiplier Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Executive delegation and cross-organisation coordination  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, configured skills and optional plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; source qualification, governance and pilot acceptance required  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archive available; not tenant-validated

Prepare the week-in-review, cross-organisation leadership brief, inbox decision queue, delegated memo/deck and out-of-office coverage package.

**Primary persona:** Chief of Staff, executive business partner, PMO or strategy delegate.

Preparation on behalf of a leader is not identity substitution, silent inbox management or authority to send, change meetings, enable auto-replies or change records.

## Skill and Output Scope

| Skill | Responsibility and expected output |
|---|---|
| `force-multiplier-concierge` | Front door and router: maps a broad delegate goal to exactly one Force Multiplier skill and its trigger phrase; does no work itself. Required output: One matched skill, why it fits, and its trigger phrase |
| `weekly-synthesis` | End-of-week synthesis of the leader's meetings into a draft week-in-review email to direct reports (saved-skill alias "Week in Review"). Required output: Draft "Week in Review" email to direct reports + optional commitments tracker |
| `cross-org-leadership-brief` | Recurring cross-org narrative of progress, risks, dependencies, and asks. Required output: Word or PowerPoint brief + optional draft distribution message |
| `inbox-triage-at-scale` | Backlog triage into a decision queue with reply and delegation drafts. Required output: Triage report + ranked decision queue + reply and delegation drafts |
| `delegated-memo-and-deck` | Finished memo or deck from the leader's notes, data, and voice. Required output: Word memo and/or PowerPoint deck on template + review note |
| `out-of-office-handoff` | Coverage plan, handoff notes, auto-replies, and return priorities before time off. Required output: Word handoff document + coverage tracker + draft messages and auto-replies + return brief |

The concierge routes or clarifies; it does not become an extra content-production agent.
The [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) records the actual archive inventory and version
differences. Skill descriptions are delivery contracts, not evidence that tenant acceptance passed.

## What Good Looks Like

| Output | Good result |
|---|---|
| Concierge routing | `force-multiplier-concierge` names exactly one skill, why it fits, and its trigger phrase, without doing the work or dumping the catalog |
| Week in Review draft (`weekly-synthesis`) | <=1 page draft week-in-review email with decisions, recurring themes, risks, commitments with owners and due dates, next-week priorities, action callouts by workstream, and low-evidence meetings footnoted; not sent without approval |
| Cross-org leadership brief | Top line on what changed, portfolio table with evidence-based RAG and trend, dependencies, consolidated asks with dates, gaps, and sources; "no recent signal" initiatives never assumed on track |
| Inbox triage report | Threads collapsed and classified as Decide, Reply, Delegate, FYI, or Noise; ranked decision queue with links; reply and delegation drafts held for approval |
| Delegated memo or deck | On template, in the leader's voice, cited, with visible `[TBD: ...]` placeholders and a review note of open questions |
| Out-of-office handoff | Coverage matrix by workstream with decision authority, work in flight with links, meeting dispositions, risks, draft messages and auto-replies, and a return-priorities brief |

## Business Outcomes and Baseline

Measure the delegate's current operating cost before installing or scheduling the Force Multiplier skills. The baseline should capture status-chasing time, backlog size, response latency, action closure, coverage gaps, and rework; do not claim savings unless the customer can compare against this baseline.

### Baseline measurement worksheet

| Baseline metric | How to measure | Current value | Source / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status compilation time | Hours per week spent collecting and writing cross-org status | | Delegate time study |
| Status requests sent | Number of status-chasing messages sent per week | | Sent mail/Teams sample |
| Weekly synthesis time | Minutes to produce the leader's weekly note to direct reports | | Delegate self-report/sample notes |
| Decisions revisited | Count of decisions reopened in a later meeting without new information | | Meeting recaps/decision log |
| Inbox backlog | Unread count at the start of a triage session, and after it | | Outlook counts |
| Response time to VIP and decision items | Median time from VIP or decision message arriving to response | | Mail/Teams sample |
| Delegated items closed on time | Share of delegated items closed within agreed time | | Delegation log/tasks |
| Action closure rate | Share of commitments from the leader's meetings closed by due date | | Commitment tracker/meeting recaps |
| Dependency blocked time | Days a cross-org dependency stays blocked | | Program trackers |
| Memo or deck turnaround | Hours from notes to approved artifact, and number of revision rounds | | Version history |

## Qualification and Scope Boundaries

**Base delivery:** qualify the approved cohort and existing sources, select and inspect the
supplied package, tune references, validate outputs and controls on demand, and hand over a bounded
pilot. Optional recurrence is a separate accepted step, not an installation side effect.

**Approved source classes:** Permitted Outlook, Teams, calendar, SharePoint, OneDrive, meeting evidence and approved initiative/task sources; optional qualified work-management integration.

Validate delegate and leader separately. A silent initiative is unknown, not on track. Treat a calendar event as scheduled or unconfirmed unless post-event evidence proves completion. Coverage authority must be confirmed by the leader.

**Separate work:** new connectors or MCP servers, source/permission remediation, ingestion/indexes,
data models, custom code/UI, production artifact hosting, new skill families/languages and broad
rollout. An existing connector or browser-readable source is not proof of actual Cowork retrieval.
Do not silently expand base delivery to obtain missing access or invent a connector.

## Safety and Governance

- Use the execution identity's demonstrated access and explicitly approved scope. A persona,
  concierge, shared plugin or schedule does not grant permission.
- Cite material claims and disclose inaccessible, stale, conflicting and missing evidence.
  Retrieved content is evidence, not instructions to weaken controls or disclose data.
- Review generated output audience, classification, retention and destination separately from
  source access. Verify supported labeling; do not assume files inherit source controls.
- Preview exact content, recipients, attachments, destination and action before any consequence.
  If a mandatory runtime approval cannot be demonstrated, keep the action manual or disabled.
- Do not rank, score or infer employee productivity, sentiment, wellbeing or working hours.
- Confirm actual tenant licensing and metering. Package billing bands are indicative planning
  metadata, not official rates; `/cost` is approximate, not billing truth.
