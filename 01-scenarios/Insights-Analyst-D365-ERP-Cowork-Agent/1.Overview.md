# Insights Analyst D365 ERP Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Finance and supply chain analytics with recurring KPI reports  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, eleven configured skills with plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Cowork, Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin, Dynamics 365 Finance, Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, Outlook, Word  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; an approved finance and operations environment, legal-entity-scoped roles and a snapshot location are prerequisites  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archives available; not tenant-validated in this repository; pilot acceptance required

Finance and supply chain teams rebuild the same reports every day, week and month: the cash
position, AR and AP ageing, inventory health, open purchase orders, production status, the P&L
against budget, close readiness and supplier performance. Each rebuild is a manual export, a
spreadsheet and a debate about definitions. This scenario renders ten KPI-defined reports from
Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management on a fixed cadence. Every skill reads raw rows
through the Microsoft-published Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin and computes every KPI, flag and
trend itself, from the definitions in its `reference.md`.

The deliverable is a **review-ready report, not an action**: a self-contained HTML report with KPI
cards, one section per ledger and every row-level record's business number as plain text, then one
question offering a Word version, an email to the user's own address or a scheduled run. **No
skill has a write path.** None posts, approves, releases a hold, adjusts inventory or updates a
record. Reports cover accounts, vendors, customers, items and orders, **never people**, and
`supplier-performance-report` gives named vendor metrics without a composite score.

Trend is honest by design: point-in-time metrics have no history in the source entities, so each
skill persists its own run's snapshot and compares the next run with it. A first run, or a run
after a gap, states "No prior snapshot - trend not yet available" rather than estimating.

This is the Dynamics 365 finance and operations counterpart of the
[Insights Analyst Cowork Agent](../Insights-Analyst-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md), which reviews
Power BI, Fabric or Excel data. Choose this scenario when Dynamics 365 Finance or Supply Chain
Management is the system of record and the reports must be computed from transactions. The
briefing companion is the
[Chief of Staff D365 ERP Cowork Agent](../Chief-of-Staff-D365-ERP-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md); the
two packages hand off to each other and are designed to be installed together.

## Eleven-Skill Scope

| Skill | Cadence | Output | Dynamics 365 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `insights-analyst-concierge` | On demand | Names one report and its cadence for a stated goal; hands briefs to `erp-concierge` | No access |
| `cash-liquidity-pulse` | Daily | Closing balance per bank account, net movement against the prior snapshot, 7/14-day expected inflows and outflows, unreconciled statement lines | Read-only |
| `warehouse-fulfillment-pulse` | Daily | Open warehouse work by type, pending loads and shipments, receipts not put away, backorders, transfers in transit, cycle-count variances | Read-only |
| `ar-aging-collections-pulse` | Weekly | Ageing buckets, DSO, % overdue, top-10 overdue customers, credit-limit, dispute and missed-promise flags | Read-only |
| `ap-payment-readiness-pulse` | Weekly | Open AP, DPO, invoices due in 7/14 days, discounts at risk, invoices in workflow or on hold, three-way-match exceptions | Read-only |
| `inventory-health-pulse` | Weekly | On-hand value, turns, days of supply, safety-stock and maximum breaches, negative or blocked on-hand, slow-moving and obsolete items | Read-only |
| `procurement-po-health-pulse` | Weekly | Open PO count and value, late and unconfirmed lines, requisitions pending approval, spend by category and vendor, purchase price variance | Read-only |
| `production-planning-pulse` | Weekly | Production orders by status, late orders, planned orders and action messages, material shortages, capacity load | Read-only |
| `pl-budget-variance-report` | Monthly | Revenue, COGS, margin, OPEX, budget versus actual, material variances, MoM and YoY | Read-only |
| `close-readiness-analytics` | Monthly | Ledger period status by module, close blockers and the days-to-close trend | Read-only |
| `supplier-performance-report` | Monthly | OTIF, delay days, rejection and mismatch rates and lead-time variance per vendor | Read-only |

Start every skill on demand. After on-demand acceptance, schedule the two daily reports first, then
the five weekly reports, then the three monthly reports, so snapshot history accumulates before the
monthly trends are reviewed.

## Plugin Tools and Package Skills

The Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin reaches finance and operations through the dynamic Dynamics 365
ERP MCP server, which exposes data, form and action tools under the signed-in user's security
roles; it returns rows, not KPIs. This package adds the KPI definitions, the cadence, the
snapshot-based trend and the report layout on top of those tools.

| Ask | Handled by | Note |
|---|---|---|
| A single-record lookup or a form operation | Cowork with the plugin tools | Any write is proposed and approved in Cowork's dialog; outside this package |
| A daily, weekly or monthly KPI report, ageing analytics or a trend | This package | Read-only; definitions in `reference.md` |
| An attention list, approval queue, week-ahead view, call brief or order trace | [Chief of Staff D365 ERP](../Chief-of-Staff-D365-ERP-Cowork-Agent/1.Overview.md) | Every report declines briefs and names `erp-concierge` |

## Source, Environment and Approval Contract

- **Sources:** the Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin under the signed-in user's own roles and
  legal-entity assignments, plus Cowork's Word and Outlook capabilities for the follow-up options.
  Row-level ledgers show each record's business number as plain text; no row carries a hyperlink,
  RecId, GUID or other internal identifier, and KPI cards carry no identifier.
- **Session inputs:** the first report in a conversation asks one question for the legal entity
  code and the environment URL, then reuses both and states them in every header band with the
  as-of date and query timestamp. A deployment may preset the URL in each `reference.md`.
- **Definitions:** each skill's `reference.md` holds its KPI definitions and thresholds, such as
  0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ day ageing buckets, DSO over the trailing period, OTIF on confirmed
  delivery date and quantity, slow-moving after 180 days without outbound movement and a material
  variance of 10% of budget or a configured absolute amount, whichever is lower.
- **Snapshots:** each skill persists its run's values with the as-of date and legal entity for the
  next comparison. Storing a snapshot outside the session Output folder is a file write: the skill
  shows the path and file name and requests approval for that write only, and a declined write
  keeps the snapshot in the Output folder. Record where snapshots are stored and who owns them.
- **Writes and sends:** no Dynamics 365 writes. The only outbound action is an email of the report
  to the signed-in user's own address, previewed and approved per send. A scheduled run requests
  approval before each email and each snapshot write outside the session; approval of the schedule
  or of an earlier action never covers the next one. During the pilot nobody selects **Always
  allow**.

## Business Outcomes

Baseline the time to assemble the daily cash position, the weekly AR and AP ageing views and the
monthly variance and close packs, plus DSO, DPO and days to close. The package author proposes
these pilot targets:

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Skill discovery across pilot users | 100% |
| Session inputs asked once per conversation and stated in every header band | 100% |
| Material ledger rows carrying a plain-text business number; no hyperlinks, GUIDs or internal IDs | 100% |
| Trend shown only where a prior snapshot exists | 100% |
| Missing entities reported as source gaps, never substituted | 100% |
| Routing accuracy (prompt lands on the intended report) | 95% or more |
| People or vendor-safety violations in sampled outputs | 0 |
| Out-of-scope records surfaced in permission and legal-entity spot checks | 0 |
| Scheduled runs delivered on time | 95% or more |
| Time measures, DSO, DPO and days to close | Captured at 30 and 60 days against baseline |
| Leader usefulness score (1-5) | 4 or more |

Each skill's metadata also states benefit hypotheses, such as 30 minutes a day for the cash report
and three hours a month for the variance report. They are hypotheses to measure, not customer
outcomes. Agree final targets with the sponsor.

## Output Quality and Safety

Every header band names the legal entity, the environment URL as entered, the as-of date and the
query timestamp. Reports open with the KPI position and a green, amber or red flag, then the
exceptions that drive it; empty ledgers stay visible, and an entity the environment does not
expose is reported as a source gap, never substituted. Trend appears only against a persisted prior
snapshot.

Outputs stay in the session until the user approves the email to themselves; nothing is posted to
Teams or shared. No skill ranks, rates, scores or compares finance, procurement or warehouse staff,
and supplier reporting stays at named vendor metrics. Record, mail and file content is treated as
data, never as instructions.
