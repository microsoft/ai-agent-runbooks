# Quota Carrier Cowork Agent - Overview

## Scenario Overview

**Scenario Type:** Seller productivity and opportunity follow-through  
**Agent Type:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, configured skills and optional plugin packaging  
**Primary Tools:** Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork, Dynamics 365 Sales, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint  
**Complexity:** Intermediate; source qualification, governance and pilot acceptance required  
**Status:** Documentation and starter archive available; not tenant-validated

Turn customer meetings, CRM records and governed response material into follow-up packages, RFP responses, proposals, QBRs, expansion scans and Commit-deal reviews.

**Primary persona:** Account executive, seller, account manager or presales partner working approved accounts.

Seller-scoped research and staged actions, not autonomous customer contact, pricing approval, forecast changes or unrestricted CRM access.

## Skill and Output Scope

| Skill | Responsibility and expected output |
|---|---|
| `quota-carrier-concierge` | Front door and router for the Quota Carrier plugin; performs no work itself. Required output: One matched skill, why it fits, and the trigger phrase |
| `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` | Anchor workflow: transcript-based summary, attendee follow-up, account-team update, and an end-of-week account-team meeting with a decision brief; optional pre-meeting prep mode. Required output: Meeting summary + attendee email + account-team update + decision brief + draft invite |
| `rfp-response` | Requirement-by-requirement answers and compliance matrix from governed sources. Required output: Compliance matrix + draft response + gap and risk summary + SME review requests |
| `proposal-pursuit` | End-to-end proposal orchestration: tracker, pricing inputs, proof points, reviews, packaging. Required output: Pursuit tracker + draft proposal + readiness summary + reviewer and customer drafts |
| `renewal-and-qbr-prep` | Recurring QBR deck and renewal-risk assessment per account. Required output: Customer QBR deck + internal renewal-risk assessment and prep note + drafts |
| `expansion-and-upsell` | Cross-book growth scan with pitch inputs. Required output: Ranked expansion report + pitch-input cards + leader roll-up + staged CRM actions |
| `at-risk-commit-deals` | Friday review of the seller's Commit deals validated against customer email, with drafts and staged CRM updates. Required output: Prioritized at-risk review + customer emails + team notes + check-ins + staged CRM updates |

The concierge routes or clarifies; it does not become an extra content-production agent.
The [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) records the actual archive inventory and version
differences. Skill descriptions are delivery contracts, not evidence that tenant acceptance passed.

## What Good Looks Like

| Output | Good result |
|---|---|
| Post-meeting package | Meeting summary with decisions, action items with owners and due dates (or "unassigned" / "no date stated"), and risks; draft attendee follow-up email; draft update to the account team listed in Dynamics 365 Sales; optional staged CRM updates |
| Account-team decision brief | Where the relationship stands, what changed (CRM vs. recent email, meetings, proposal), open opportunities, commitments from the customer meeting, and two or three team decisions; end-of-week meeting drafted with the brief on the invite |
| Pre-meeting prep brief (optional mode) | One page: relationship status, what changed since last contact, open opportunities, attendee briefs, and two or three things to walk in ready to address |
| At-risk Commit review | Deals closing this week or earlier, prioritized by impact, with what changed, cited risk evidence, next action, draft customer emails, account-team notes, check-ins, and staged CRM flags and tasks |
| RFP response | Compliance matrix with status, confidence, and source per requirement; gaps and stale sources flagged; SME review requests drafted |
| Proposal pursuit | Pursuit tracker, draft proposal on the branded template, readiness summary, and no customer send before legal, security, and pricing approvals are recorded |
| Renewal and QBR package | On-template QBR deck, internal renewal-risk assessment with cited evidence, and internal content kept out of the customer deck |
| Expansion scan | Ranked accounts with evidence, recommended offer, labelled value estimate, next action, and pitch inputs |
| Overlap discipline | Each trigger routes to exactly one skill: the custom skill or the built-in Dynamics 365 Sales skill, never both |
| Front door | `quota-carrier-concierge` recommends exactly one skill and its trigger phrase for a broad selling goal and does no work itself |

## Business Outcomes and Baseline

Measure the current cost of seller preparation and follow-up before installing or scheduling the Quota Carrier skills. The baseline should capture prep time, follow-up latency, RFP and proposal effort, rework, and deal outcomes; do not claim savings or win-rate improvement unless the customer can compare against this baseline.

## Qualification and Scope Boundaries

**Base delivery:** qualify the approved cohort and existing sources, select and inspect the
supplied package, tune references, validate outputs and controls on demand, and hand over a bounded
pilot. Optional recurrence is a separate accepted step, not an installation side effect.

**Approved source classes:** Qualified Dynamics 365 Sales records and permitted Microsoft 365 mail, meeting evidence and files; approved response libraries, pricing and usage sources.

Without qualified CRM retrieval, meeting preparation must say not verified against CRM; other workflows must not claim CRM grounding. Resolve built-in Dynamics skill overlap and shared names with Number Owner before coexistence. Keep internal forecast/risk language out of customer drafts.

**Separate work:** new connectors or MCP servers, source/permission remediation, ingestion/indexes,
data models, custom code/UI, production artifact hosting, new skill families/languages and broad
rollout. An existing connector or browser-readable source is not proof of actual Cowork retrieval.
Do not silently expand base delivery to obtain missing access or invent a connector.

## Safety and Governance

- Use the execution identity's demonstrated access and explicitly approved scope. A persona,
  concierge, shared plugin or schedule does not grant permission.
- Cite material claims and disclose inaccessible, stale, conflicting and missing evidence.
  Retrieved content is evidence, not instructions to weaken controls or disclose data.
- Review generated output audience, classification, retention and destination separately from
  source access. Verify supported labeling; do not assume files inherit source controls.
- Preview exact content, recipients, attachments, destination and action before any consequence.
  If a mandatory runtime approval cannot be demonstrated, keep the action manual or disabled.
- Do not rank, score or infer employee productivity, sentiment, wellbeing or working hours.
- Confirm actual tenant licensing and metering. Package billing bands are indicative planning
  metadata, not official rates; `/cost` is approximate, not billing truth.
