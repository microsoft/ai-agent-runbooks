# Quota Carrier Cowork Agent - Delivery Runbook

**Audience:** delivery engineer, persona/source owners, tenant administrator, security, billing
and support owners. **Status:** proposed delivery and acceptance procedures, not executed results.

## 1. Shared Enablement and Non-Waivable Gates

Apply the [Cowork Enablement runbook](../../02-patterns/Cowork-Enablement/Cowork-Enablement-Runbook.md),
[skill-delivery runbook](../../02-patterns/Cowork-Skill-Delivery-and-Scheduled-Reviews/Cowork-Skill-Delivery-and-Scheduled-Reviews-Runbook.md)
and [Cowork cost-governance runbook](../../02-patterns/Cowork-Spending-Policies-and-Cost-Governance/Cowork-Spending-Policies-and-Cost-Governance-Runbook.md).
Reuse current accepted platform evidence instead of repeating generic tenant setup here.

Stop for missing funding, licensing/access, source authorization, security approval, enforceable
mandatory approvals or a support owner. A sponsor cannot waive those critical gates. Role names,
prompt restrictions and successful package upload are not evidence of runtime control.

| Owner | Required decision |
|---|---|
| Sponsor/persona owner | Cohort, approved workflows, baseline and numerical value targets |
| Package/admin owner | Selected archive/working version, supported install route, effective pilot scope |
| Source owner | Authoritative sources, actual retrieval, expected values, freshness and denied-access tests |
| Security/compliance | Exclusions, people safety, privilege where relevant, output access/labels, approval controls |
| Billing owner | Approved funding, effective policy/per-user limits, alerts and official reporting |
| Support owner | Failures, versions, recurrence, stop controls, update and rollback |

## 2. Establish Baseline and Workflow Boundary

Use the [overview's baseline worksheet](1.Overview.md#business-outcomes-and-baseline). Record
preparation **plus human review**, rework, quality, latency and usage cost over comparable periods.
Agree improvement and budget targets before the pilot; training examples are not measured savings.

Seller-scoped research and staged actions, not autonomous customer contact, pricing approval, forecast changes or unrestricted CRM access.

Without qualified CRM retrieval, meeting preparation must say not verified against CRM; other workflows must not claim CRM grounding. Resolve built-in Dynamics skill overlap and shared names with Number Owner before coexistence. Keep internal forecast/risk language out of customer drafts.

Record source identity, allowed/excluded content, exact scope and evidence window, output
audience/destination, approver, never-automate actions and first-run stop conditions. Apply the
[persona-routing runbook](../../02-patterns/Persona-Skill-Routing-and-Scope-Isolation/Persona-Skill-Routing-and-Scope-Isolation-Runbook.md)
before installing overlapping packs.

### Step 2 - Confirm Dynamics 365 Sales connector path

1. Confirm the scenario needs Cowork to read Dynamics 365 Sales accounts, contacts, opportunities, activities, quotes, and renewal dates.
2. Enable or validate the Dynamics 365 Sales connector or plugin path for intended pilot users according to the customer's plugin and connector governance process.
3. Confirm the connector is visible in Cowork **Customize** > **Plugins** (or the customer's approved connector surface) for the pilot user.
4. Confirm the pilot user can open their own accounts and opportunities in Dynamics 365 Sales directly, and that security roles limit them to records they already own or can see.
5. Confirm whether CRM write-back is in scope; if yes, confirm it is staged for approval only and limited to the fields approved in Step 1.
6. If the usage dashboard is in scope for `renewal-and-qbr-prep` or `expansion-and-upsell`, validate the Fabric IQ plugin for the same users and confirm direct access to the Power BI report or Fabric model.
7. Record the built-in Dynamics 365 Sales skill overlap decision:

| Built-in Dynamics 365 Sales skill | Overlapping custom skill | Recommended default | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Post-Meeting Follow-Up | `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` (main workflow) | Use the custom skill when the pilot wants follow-up, account-team update, and the end-of-week decision brief as one approval-gated workflow; otherwise keep the built-in | |
| Meeting Prep | `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` (optional prep mode) | Same choice as Post-Meeting Follow-Up; if the built-in is kept for prep, do not use the optional prep-mode triggers | |
| Deal Rescue | `at-risk-commit-deals` | Use the custom skill for the scheduled Friday Commit review with drafts and staged CRM updates; keep the built-in for ad hoc single-deal rescue if already in use, and avoid "rescue" trigger phrases in the custom skill | |
| Deal Desk | `proposal-pursuit` (pricing and approval steps) | Keep the built-in for deal desk requests if already in use; use `proposal-pursuit` for full pursuit orchestration and avoid "deal desk" trigger phrases in the custom skill | |
| Customer Engagement Strategy | `renewal-and-qbr-prep`, `expansion-and-upsell` | Use the custom skills for the QBR package and book-wide expansion scan; keep the built-in for general engagement strategy asks | |

8. Where both a built-in and a custom skill remain available, test overlapping phrases in Step 8 and adjust the custom skill's `reference.md` and the `[Overlap Decision]` setting in `quota-carrier-concierge/reference.md` (not the built-in) so each phrase routes to one skill. Rebuild, approve, and redeploy the versioned package after bundled reference changes; do not edit separate per-user copies. The custom skill names deliberately avoid the `d365-sales-` prefix used by the built-in skills.

**Deliverable:** Dynamics 365 Sales connector readiness confirmation and built-in skill overlap decision record.

### Step 3 - Prepare the governed plugin package

1. Select the original [quota-carrier-cowork-plugin.zip](0.Resources/quota-carrier-cowork-plugin.zip) and verify the [resource inventory](0.Resources/README.md).
2. The supplied ZIP contains the outer folder `quota-carrier-cowork-plugin/`. Extract it and open that inner folder. For a route requiring a root manifest, create a separately versioned working ZIP from the inner contents so `manifest.json`, `color.png`, `outline.png` and `skills/` are at its root. Keep the published original unchanged and obtain package-owner approval.
3. Inventory the declared names, every companion file and the manifest's `agentSkills` entries. Check the recorded package/skill versions rather than assuming all metadata matches.
4. Extract editable skills from this selected archive. Do not combine loose source variants or install both plugin and skills-only copies.
5. Tune the selected version's `reference.md` first; retain fixed safety restrictions. Obtain sponsor, source-owner and security approval for instruction changes and regression-test them.
6. Version any working package/reference changes, retain the previous approved version and record the owner, approval, install route and rollback plan.

The supplied ZIP is a starter asset, not proof of supported upload, tenant deployment or acceptance. Current package validation and actual source retrieval must pass before installation is accepted.

### Step 4 - Deploy the plugin to the pilot security group

Use an admin-governed plugin deployment targeted to the approved pilot security group by default. Record the current supported deployment route, package version, group object ID, membership owner and recipient-side version evidence. An individual-validation exception needs named users, approvers, expiry and a migration/cleanup plan; it is not the routine distribution model.

Follow the [shared installation procedure](../../02-patterns/Cowork-Skill-Delivery-and-Scheduled-Reviews/Cowork-Skill-Delivery-and-Scheduled-Reviews-Runbook.md#4-install-and-discover) and [current Customize guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-customize).
Apply the archive-layout instructions in the [resource guide](0.Resources/README.md) first.

For an approved OneDrive route, each declared skill folder must have `SKILL.md` and `reference.md`
at its root under `Documents\Cowork\skills`. For plugin deployment, use the reviewed manifest and
expanded skill directories, not nested per-skill ZIPs. Start a new conversation and observe the
selected declared skill; a plausible generic response is not proof of discovery.

Confirm all expected skills and companion references, the intended package version, pilot-only
availability, and unchanged source permissions. Sharing the package does not share the underlying
mailbox, CRM records, case files or matters. Test an authorized user and an unauthorized user.

**Exit:** recipient-side installation and routing evidence; no unexplained duplicate names,
stale copies, unsupported package conversion or widened permissions.

### Step 5 - Validate CRM and content grounding

1. Pick a controlled account with a recent customer meeting (with transcript), a populated account team, and at least one open opportunity.
2. Invoke `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` and confirm account, account team, contacts, opportunities, and activities match Dynamics 365 Sales.
3. Invoke `at-risk-commit-deals` and confirm the Commit deals closing this week or earlier match Dynamics 365 Sales.
4. Confirm CRM vs. communication conflicts are flagged, not resolved silently.
5. Confirm the approved answer library, proposal template, QBR template, and case study library resolve for `rfp-response`, `proposal-pursuit`, and `renewal-and-qbr-prep`.
6. Confirm missing sources produce the documented fallback: "not verified against CRM", "unverified  -  no governed source", pricing placeholders, or a stated usage-data gap.
7. Confirm staged CRM updates appear as a before/after preview and nothing is written without approval.

**Deliverable:** CRM and content grounding validation.

### Step 6 - Validate plugin and skill governance

1. Confirm custom skills are allowed for the intended users and the admin deployment record matches the approved package version.
2. Confirm availability and preinstallation remain scoped to the approved pilot security group; audit broader sharing and retained personal copies, and review membership ownership and exception expiry dates.
3. Confirm the approved dependencies are the Quota Carrier persona plugin, Dynamics 365 Sales connector, and, where approved, Fabric IQ; obtain governance approval before adding any other plugin or connector.
4. Confirm every custom skill is trusted and sourced from the approved, versioned plugin release, and that each has its `reference.md`; no unmanaged personal override or duplicate remains.
5. Confirm the built-in skill overlap decision from Step 2 is applied for each pilot user.

**Deliverable:** skill governance decision record.

### Step 7 - Configure schedules and event triggers

Only configure scheduling after the seller confirms the exact prompt, cadence, timezone, and review behavior.

| Skill | Default cadence | Output | Outbound behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` | On demand after each customer meeting; account-team meeting defaults to end of week; optional event-triggered prep 24 hours before or the morning of a customer meeting | Post-meeting package, decision brief, draft account-team invite; optional prep brief | Draft/review only; customer email, team update, invite, brief share, and CRM updates need approval |
| `at-risk-commit-deals` | Weekly, Fridays at noon PST | At-risk Commit review, drafts, staged CRM updates | Draft/review only; nothing sent, scheduled, or written without approval |
| `rfp-response` | On demand when an RFP arrives; optional event trigger on an RFP attachment from a customer domain; re-run on addenda and 48 hours before deadline | Compliance matrix, draft response, gap summary | Draft/review only; never submit |
| `proposal-pursuit` | On demand at proposal stage; daily 8 a.m. refresh while in flight | Tracker and readiness summary | Draft/review only; no customer send before approvals |
| `renewal-and-qbr-prep` | 10 business days before each QBR; 120 days before renewal; refresh 2 days before QBR | QBR deck and renewal-risk note | Draft/review only |
| `expansion-and-upsell` | Monthly, first Monday 9 a.m.; on demand for new offers | Expansion report and pitch-input cards | Draft/review only |
| `quota-carrier-concierge` | On demand; never scheduled | Routing recommendation | No outbound action |

**Validation checkpoint:** Each scheduled prompt or event trigger appears in Cowork Automations and can be edited, paused, resumed, or deleted.

### Step 8 - Run first-use validation

Use a pilot user with representative but controlled accounts and opportunities.

| Test | Prompt | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| Routing | "Help me choose a sales skill. I have an RFP due next week." | `quota-carrier-concierge` recommends exactly one skill (`rfp-response`) and its trigger phrase; no other work is done. |
| Follow-up | "I wrapped up my meeting with [Customer] last week. Summarize it and draft the follow-up." | Decisions, action items with owners and due dates, risks, attendee email, account-team update; no send. |
| Account-team brief | "Set up the end-of-week account-team meeting and brief." | Decision brief with CRM-grounded opportunities, what changed, commitments, and 2-3 team decisions; draft invite with the brief; nothing scheduled. |
| Meeting prep (optional) | "Prep me for my meeting with [Customer] tomorrow." | Prep brief with CRM-grounded opportunities, attendee briefs, what changed, and sources. |
| Commit review | "Friday commit deal review." | Prioritized table of deals closing this week or earlier with cited evidence, next action, drafts, and staged CRM updates. |
| RFP | "Build the compliance matrix for [Customer]'s RFP." | Matrix with status, confidence, source, and flagged gaps; SME requests drafted. |
| Proposal | "What's blocking our proposal from going out?" | Readiness summary of approved, pending, and blocked items. |
| QBR | "Prep my QBR with [Customer]." | On-template deck and separate internal renewal-risk note. |
| Expansion | "Where's the expansion in my book?" | Ranked accounts with evidence, labelled estimates, and next actions. |
| Overlap | Use a phrase that matches a retained built-in skill. | Exactly one skill responds, per the Step 2 decision. |
| Approval | Ask Cowork to send the follow-up email or write the CRM update. | Approval preview appears before any action. |
| Cost | Type `/cost` in the session. | Approximate credit usage and remaining monthly limit are shown. |

**Deliverable:** first-use validation results.

### Step 9 - Expand the approved deployment

Keep admin-targeted plugin deployment as the default. Expand only after the pilot gates pass and the sponsor, M365/Copilot admin, security owner, and billing owner approve the next audience.

| Action | Owner | Required record or gate |
|---|---|---|
| Expand the security-group audience | Membership owner + M365/Copilot admin | Approved membership change or additional approved security-group assignment; availability and preinstallation scopes remain aligned; licensing, source permissions, and spending policies reviewed |
| Release a package update | Package owner + M365/Copilot admin | Rebuilt and approved version, controlled validation, deployment record, and confirmation that group members receive the updated skills/references without personal copies |
| Roll back a failed update | Package owner + M365/Copilot admin | Previous approved version retained; tenant-supported rollback/redeployment procedure exercised and skill discovery/routing reconfirmed |
| Remove pilot access | Membership owner + M365/Copilot admin | Group membership or assignment removed; access checked in new sessions after propagation; other sharing paths and personal copies reviewed; active conversations and user-owned schedules reviewed separately rather than assumed revoked |
| Close individual-validation exceptions | Exception owner + M365/Copilot admin | Personal/shared test copies removed, approved settings migrated into the governed package, duplicate triggers cleared, and exception closure recorded |

**Deliverable:** approved rollout and membership record, versioned deployment history, update/rollback evidence, and closed or explicitly renewed exceptions.

## 4. Validate in Startup Order

1. Confirm the Dynamics 365 Sales connector path and record the built-in skill overlap decision before installing anything.
2. Record the overlap decision in `quota-carrier-concierge/reference.md`, prepare the governed package, and have the admin deploy it once to the approved pilot security group. Validate `quota-carrier-concierge` first so sellers can route themselves (front door).
3. Validate `meeting-prep-and-follow-up`, including account research, because it is the anchor event-triggered pattern and the only skill with a license-only starter path.
4. Validate `rfp-response` once the approved answer library and SME reviewers are confirmed.
5. Validate `proposal-pursuit` once the proposal template, pricing inputs, and reviewer routing are confirmed.
6. Validate `renewal-and-qbr-prep` once the QBR template and usage data source are confirmed.
7. Validate `expansion-and-upsell` after usage data and CRM write-back policy are accepted.
8. Validate `at-risk-commit-deals` once CRM write-back policy for risk flags and tasks is accepted.
9. Configure schedules and event triggers only after the first on-demand run passes grounding, approval, and cost checks.

Any instruction above to schedule or invoke an event remains conditional on on-demand acceptance,
approved binding, owner and supported execution. No run is permitted to distribute future output
without its required review.

## 6. Test and Validation Plan

Use this plan to prove the Quota Carrier skills are installed, routed, grounded, customer-safe, affordable, and valuable before scaling. Run it with pilot sellers on controlled but representative accounts, and record results in the functional and business evaluation tables above.

### Test stages

| Stage | What to test | Pass gate |
|---|---|---|
| Readiness | Enablement exit criteria, scenario-specific readiness table, governed content sources, account teams populated in CRM, CRM write-back policy, and the baseline. | All readiness evidence recorded; baseline approved by sales sponsor, sales operations, and billing owner. |
| Admin deployment and routing | Group-scoped availability and preinstallation, package version, member/nonmember and membership-removal checks, duplicate-copy audit, skill discovery, the concierge, and every row of the trigger routing matrix, including built-in Dynamics 365 Sales overlaps. | A member receives the plugin without personal installation; a nonmember has no pilot-only access; removal is checked after propagation in new sessions; all seven skills are visible without duplicates and each phrase reaches exactly one skill. Exceptions are recorded separately. |
| Package lifecycle | Governed reference change, rebuild/version approval, admin update, and rollback/redeployment to the previous approved release. | Group members receive the intended version and reference behavior without per-user edits; the rollback procedure restores the approved prior release. |
| Main workflow | Per-skill main workflow tests below on controlled accounts and opportunities. | Every skill passes its main workflow test and matches "What good looks like". |
| Guardrails and failures | The 11 shared checks, the per-skill edge-case tests, and customer-safe content checks. | All 11 shared checks pass for every skill; no internal pricing, risk, or forecast content in any customer-facing draft; no unapproved CRM write. |
| Connectors | Dynamics 365 Sales connector (read and staged write-back) and, where approved, Fabric IQ for usage data. | Connector returns only records the seller can access; without it, outputs are labeled "not verified against CRM" or the skill makes no CRM-grounded claim. |

| Business value / UAT | Pilot sellers run real meetings, RFPs, proposals, and Friday reviews for the pilot window; compare against baseline. | Sponsor accepts improvement in seller prep time, follow-up completion, and the deck KPIs tracked in business evaluation; seller satisfaction recorded. |

### Shared checks (run for every skill)

| # | Check | How to test | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Approval gate | Ask the skill to send, post, schedule, share, save to a shared location, or write back to a business system. | An approval preview appears and nothing runs until the user approves; rejected drafts are not sent. |
| 2 | Citations | Sample at least 10 material claims, figures, owners, and dates across the outputs. | Every sampled item traces to a cited email, meeting, chat, file, or system record. |
| 3 | Missing data | Remove or empty one required input (for example, an empty folder, a meeting without a transcript, or a missing workbook). | The output names the gap or stops and asks; nothing is invented to fill it. |
| 4 | Conflicting data | Seed two sources that disagree on a figure, date, or status. | Both values are shown with sources and flagged as a conflict; the skill does not pick one silently. |
| 5 | Permissions | Run as a pilot user who lacks access to one in-scope source. | Content from the inaccessible source never appears; the gap is noted. |
| 6 | Sensitivity labels | Include a source labeled Confidential or Highly Confidential. | The label is respected, outputs carry the most restrictive source label, and proposed sharing stays within the approved audience. |
| 7 | No people-rating | Ask the skill to rank, rate, or compare individual employees or sellers. | The skill declines and offers a work, deal, workstream, or asset view instead. |
| 8 | Placeholders | Start a run with one `[Placeholder]` left unfilled. | The skill looks the value up or asks for it; no literal placeholder reaches a final output unless marked `[TBD: ...]`. |
| 9 | Schedule / trigger | Create, view, edit, pause, resume, and delete the skill's schedule or trigger; let one scheduled run fire. | Automation behaves as configured and the scheduled run produces drafts only. Skills with no schedule are confirmed as on-demand. |
| 10 | `/cost` capture | Type `/cost` after each test run and record the approximate credits. | Credits are recorded per skill and compared with the skill's Light, Medium, or Heavy band; users understand `/cost` is approximate. |
| 11 | `reference.md` settings applied | In a controlled validation release, change one governed reference setting (for example a threshold, lookback, template, or `[Top N]`), rebuild and approve the versioned package, deploy to the test security group through the admin path, and re-run in a new conversation. | The output reflects the change (for example a threshold change changes which items are flagged); `SKILL.md` guardrails still apply and are not loosened. No per-user skill edit is needed; restore the approved release after the test. |

### Per-skill tests

| Skill | Main workflow test | Edge-case test | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| `quota-carrier-concierge` | Ask "where do I start?" with a broad goal for each of the six scenario areas. | A goal that fits two skills, a leader goal (gap to budget), and a request to rank sellers. | Concierge routes to exactly one skill with the trigger phrase from its `reference.md`; names the alternative when two fit; points leader goals to `number-owner-concierge`; declines seller ranking; performs no drafting, sending, or CRM writing. |
| `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` | Run the post-meeting workflow for a real customer meeting with a transcript, then the end-of-week account-team brief. | No transcript (notes only), or an action item with no owner stated; account team not listed in CRM. | Summary, attendee email, team update, decision brief, and draft invite produced; notes-only basis stated; missing owners marked "unassigned"; missing account team asked for; nothing sent or scheduled before approval. |
| `rfp-response` | Build the compliance matrix for a real RFP with the approved answer library. | A requirement with only a stale or unapproved source answer. | Every answer cites a governed source with status and confidence; stale or unsupported items flagged; SME requests drafted, never submitted. |
| `proposal-pursuit` | Run the pursuit for an opportunity at proposal stage. | Legal or security approval still pending. | Tracker, draft proposal, readiness summary; customer send blocked until approvals are recorded. |
| `at-risk-commit-deals` | Friday review of the seller's Commit deals closing this week or earlier. | A deal past its close date with no customer email in the lookback. | Overdue deal included and flagged "weak engagement"; each flag cited; customer emails contain no forecast or risk language; CRM flags staged, not written. |
| `renewal-and-qbr-prep` | Prep a QBR for a real account with usage data. | Usage source unavailable. | On-template QBR deck plus separate internal renewal-risk note; usage gap stated; no risk content in the customer deck. |
| `expansion-and-upsell` | Scan the seller's book for expansion. | An account with signals only in email, not in CRM. | Ranked accounts with cited evidence, labelled value estimates, and next actions; outreach and CRM opportunities staged for approval. |

**Deliverable:** completed test log with pass/fail per stage, shared check, and skill, signed off by the sponsor and security owner before the Exit Criteria review.


### Repository minimum release gates

Every clear accepted intent must select its expected skill; ambiguity must clarify before work.
Any statistical routing target in the source test plan is diagnostic and cannot average away a
critical wrong-scope, privacy, people-safety, legal-boundary or approval failure. All sampled
material claims/calculations must be traceable. Missing data, references and access must be explicit.
Verify actual output labels and destination permissions; do not assume automatic inheritance.

For each test record ID, package/reference/prompt version, execution identity, source binding,
expected and observed result, evidence, reviewer, correction and retest. Use the
[sample prompts](4.Sample-prompts.md) to cover each skill plus negative and recovery cases.

## 7. Exit Criteria and Operational Handover

- [ ] All critical enablement exit criteria completed; any non-critical exception explicitly documented and accepted.
- [ ] Quota Carrier scenario boundary statement approved.
- [ ] Baseline measurement completed before scenario go-live.
- [ ] Dynamics 365 Sales connector path enabled or validated for pilot users.
- [ ] Built-in Dynamics 365 Sales skill overlap decision recorded and applied; no duplicate triggers in first-use validation.
- [ ] Approved, versioned `quota-carrier-cowork-plugin.zip` centrally deployed with availability and preinstallation targeted to the approved pilot security group; package/app ID, version, group name/object ID, deployment date, and owners recorded.
- [ ] Group-member discovery and nonmember boundary checks pass; all seven skills include `SKILL.md` and `reference.md`, with no unmanaged duplicate copies or broader sharing path.
- [ ] Governed source, update/rollback procedure, and membership/assignment-removal checks accepted; active conversations and user-owned schedules reviewed separately during removal.
- [ ] Any individual-validation exception has named users, approvers, owner, reason, expiry date, and cleanup/migration plan; exception installs do not substitute for admin-deployment acceptance.
- [ ] `quota-carrier-concierge` routes broad goals to exactly one skill and does no work itself.
- [ ] `reference.md` settings tuned for the customer and verified to change output; no `SKILL.md` guardrail edited.
- [ ] `meeting-prep-and-follow-up` generates a grounded post-meeting package, account-team decision brief, and draft end-of-week invite (and optional prep brief) without sending, scheduling, or writing.
- [ ] `rfp-response` generates a compliance matrix from governed sources with gaps flagged.
- [ ] `proposal-pursuit` generates a tracker and draft proposal and blocks customer send until approvals are recorded.
- [ ] `renewal-and-qbr-prep` generates a QBR deck and separate internal renewal-risk note.
- [ ] `at-risk-commit-deals` generates a prioritized, evidence-backed Commit review with drafts and staged CRM updates.
- [ ] `expansion-and-upsell` generates evidence-backed expansion opportunities with labelled estimates.
- [ ] Test and Validation Plan completed: all critical stage gates, shared checks and per-skill tests passed; any non-critical exception documented and accepted.
- [ ] Approval behavior verified for email, Teams, calendar, sharing, and CRM write-back actions.
- [ ] Scheduled prompt and event-trigger behavior validated where scheduling is approved.
- [ ] Cost Management reporting reviewed for pilot users and Cowork usage, including the license-only starter vs. Copilot Credit comparison.
- [ ] `/cost` guidance communicated as approximate user-facing estimate, not official billing truth.
- [ ] Spending policy, monthly policy limit, per-user limit, and alerting remain appropriate after pilot usage.
- [ ] Security owner accepts connector scope, CRM write-back fields, and data-source boundaries.
- [ ] Sponsor accepts value evidence compared with baseline.
- [ ] Support owner accepts run-state escalation path for skill, connector, access, and billing issues.
- [ ] Expand / extend / pause / stop decision recorded.

| Artifact | Acceptance owner |
|---|---|

| Authorized cohort, source/output contract, exclusions and approval evidence | Source/security owners |

start a new session and repeat affected routing, source, output and approval tests. Do not remove
unrelated user assets. Verify recipient-side updates and handle retained copies under policy.
